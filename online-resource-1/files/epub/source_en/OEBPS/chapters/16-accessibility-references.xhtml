Appendix

Accessibility, licences and references

Accessibility

The explanatory chapters are mainly text and adapt to the settings of the reading application (theme, font, line spacing, width). Every figure has a title and a description, every video a text summary and every 3D model an alternative page with an image and a description. The questions use native buttons, radio buttons and lists, for compatibility with the keyboard and assistive technologies. Version 2.1 added accessible names to the controls of the 3D models, a correct keyboard navigation order, landmarks and higher colour contrast. That version was evaluated by the author against EPUB Accessibility 1.1 and WCAG 2.1 AA (self-assessment, not third-party certification). The 3D models themselves cannot be “read” by a screen reader; their information is given in the text and on the static pages. The videos have no sound.

In the Greek version 3.0, the interactive pages were rebuilt around a semantic map linking component names and descriptions to visibility and highlighting controls, keyboard rotation and preset views. The interface provides live messages describing supported changes; their delivery must be checked in each browser, reading system and assistive technology. Colour calculations used CVD simulation and CIEDE2000 differences. For outlined planes, an opaque identifying outline can contrast with the stage even when the transparent fill remains below 3:1. These specified-colour calculations do not cover every adjacent rendered surface or establish complete WCAG conformance. Patterns and outlines provide additional cues where implemented. Static descriptions and views remain in the HTML without scripting, and the EPUB includes directly linked text alternatives. Their behaviour depends on reading-system support.

Known limitations: exploring the shape in space remains visual; a screen reader user receives descriptions, not the shape itself. The EPUB has not been tested with students who use assistive technologies.

A note on DRM

The book is distributed without DRM, in accordance with its licence. Edu Ebook Reader contains no function for decrypting or circumventing DRM and is intended for original, openly licensed or free DRM-free material.

Origin of the content

The 3D models of ethyne, the videos and the original questions are original material by the author. The Greek version and the new chapters (“σ and π bonds”, “Why hybridisation is needed”, “Comparison”, “Misconceptions”, the interactive 3D comparison and the new questions) were developed with the help of generative artificial intelligence, on the basis of interactive material that had also been produced with AI.

About this English version

This English derivative translates the Greek original, version 3.0. EPUBCheck 5.3.0, source-map consistency, script syntax, XML resources, local references and embedded model/image integrity were checked during preparation. Fresh browser, keyboard and assistive-technology runs of the English derivative have not been completed. Historical runtime observations concern the Greek originals. The authors personally checked the materials, but detailed personal results were not retained per task. No complete accessibility certification, participant usability evaluation or learning-effectiveness claim is made.
