Getting started

How to use this book

The book combines text, figures, videos, three-dimensional models and questions. Read the chapters in order and pause at the interactive elements.

After working through this book, you will be able to:

  • distinguish the σ bond (overlap along the axis) from the π bond (sideways overlap);
  • explain why the concept of hybridisation is needed;
  • describe which atomic orbitals are mixed in sp hybridisation and why the two sp orbitals form a 180° angle;
  • describe the σ bond and the two π bonds of the C≡C triple bond;
  • identify the hybridisation of each carbon atom in a simple organic compound.

What the colours in the new figures mean

Orangehybrid orbitals and σ bonds (overlap along the axis)
Blueunhybridised p orbitals and the first π bond
Greenthe second π bond, perpendicular to the first

These apply to the chapter figures and to the 3D comparison model; the 3D models of ethyne have their own legend. The colours distinguish groups of orbitals; they do not indicate electric charge.

Which reading application?

The 3D models and the questions need a reading application that allows JavaScript and WebGL, such as Edu Ebook Reader. In other applications, static images with descriptions appear in place of the 3D models, so that no content is lost.
