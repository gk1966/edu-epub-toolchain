GLB Lab 0.1 · research draft

Checks a 3D GLB model for what PAC and axe-core do not see: whether its parts have meaningful names, whether the role colours remain distinguishable under colour vision deficiency, and whether they have enough contrast against the viewer background. Processing takes place in the browser; the file is not uploaded anywhere.

4Part names and descriptions

A screen reader can announce a part only if the model or the viewer gives it a name. Names such as “Cone002” or “VMtl003” are generated by the software and convey nothing. We recommend that each part should have extras.a11y = {"label", "description"}.

Method

Colour vision deficiency simulation with the Machado, Oliveira & Fernandes (2009) matrices in linear sRGB, with interpolation between severities. CIEDE2000 colour difference according to Sharma, Wu & Dalal (2005), D65, 2° observer. The numerical core is copied unchanged from CVD Lab 2.1.

Blending with the background is computed as α·colour + (1−α)·background on 8-bit sRGB values. Scene lighting, shadows and specular highlights are not taken into account, so the values describe the material colours, not every pixel of the image. Textures (baseColorTexture) are flagged but not analysed.

The tool is neither a diagnosis nor proof that a model is understandable. It shows the teacher where to look.
