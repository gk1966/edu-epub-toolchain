Ethyne (C₂H₂)

sp Hybridisation & Bonds

σ Bonds (Axis): Each carbon atom forms 2 linear hybrid orbital lobes $sp$ ($180^\circ$). The single $\sigma_{\text{C-C}}$ bond results from the axial $sp\text{-}sp$ overlap and the two $\sigma_{\text{C-H}}$ from axial $sp\text{-}s$ overlaps.

π Bonds & Planes: The two unhybridised orbitals ($2p_y, 2p_z$) of each carbon overlap sideways in two mutually perpendicular planes, forming two independent $\pi$ bonds with increased electron density on either side of the internuclear axis.
