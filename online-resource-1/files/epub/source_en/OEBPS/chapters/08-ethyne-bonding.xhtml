Bonding in ethyne

Ethyne, C2H2, is linear. Each carbon uses one sp orbital for the bond to hydrogen and the other sp orbital for the carbon–carbon σ bond.

The C≡C triple bond

1 σ bondend-on overlap, along the internuclear axis, of two sp orbitals of the carbons
2 π bondssideways overlap of two pairs of unhybridised p orbitals; the two π bonds are perpendicular to each other
Resulta short, strong triple bond and the linear H–C≡C–H arrangement
