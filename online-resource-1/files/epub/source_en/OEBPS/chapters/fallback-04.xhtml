Linear ethyne molecule, H–C≡C–H, with a bond angle of 180° (sp hybridisation). In total, the molecule has 3 σ bonds (1 C–C and 2 C–H) and 2 π bonds. The C≡C triple bond consists of 1 σ + 2 π; the two pairs of p orbitals that form the π bonds have mutually perpendicular axes. The model shows the sp hybrid lobes along the axis and the lobes of the two π bonds, with mutually perpendicular axes.

Parts of the model

  • sp hybrid orbitals. Lobes of the sp hybrid orbitals. Each carbon has two, in a straight line at 180°; they form the σ bonds.
  • 1st π bond. One π bond: sideways overlap of one pair of unhybridised p orbitals, with lobes on both sides of the axis.
  • 2nd π bond. The second π bond, from the other pair of p orbitals. The axis of its lobes is perpendicular to the axis of the lobes of the 1st π bond.
  • Plane 1. Contains the C–C axis. It is a symmetry plane of the molecule and also the nodal plane of one of the two π bonds: the π-electron density is zero in this plane. Perpendicular to Plane 2.
  • Plane 2 (stripes). Contains the C–C axis. It is a symmetry plane of the molecule and also the nodal plane of one of the two π bonds: the π-electron density is zero in this plane. Perpendicular to Plane 1.
  • Hydrogen atoms. The two hydrogen atoms at the ends of the molecule.
  • x, y, z axes. Coordinate axes. The molecular axis is the C–C axis.
