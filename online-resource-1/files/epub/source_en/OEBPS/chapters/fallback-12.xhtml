Ethane C₂H₆

sp³ hybridisation at each carbon: tetrahedral arrangement with angles of ≈109.5° (the ideal tetrahedral angle). 7 σ bonds (1 C–C and 6 C–H) and no π bond, because no unhybridised p orbital remains. The C–C single bond (about 1.54 Å) allows rotation about the axis, with a small energy barrier; the model shows the staggered conformation. In the original model the angles were schematic; during conversion, the lobes and hydrogen atoms were placed at the hybridisation angle.

Parts of the model

  • C–C σ bond. The two sp³ lobes pointing towards each other along the C–C axis. Their end-on (axial) overlap is the σ bond, common to all three molecules; in ethane it is the only bond between the carbons (single bond).
  • sp³ hybrids towards H. The remaining sp³ hybrid orbitals: three on each carbon, one for each C–H σ bond.
  • Hydrogen atoms. The hydrogen atoms (spheres). Each is bonded to one carbon by a σ bond: overlap of a hybrid orbital with the hydrogen 1s orbital.

  • Coordinate axes. Three perpendicular axes with arrows at each carbon; one coincides with the C–C axis. The axis letters of the original model are not shown, because they were not the same in the three models.

Ethene C₂H₄

sp² hybridisation at each carbon: trigonal planar arrangement with angles of ≈120° (the ideal sp² angle; in the real molecule the angles differ slightly), with all atoms in the same plane. 5 σ bonds (1 C–C and 4 C–H) and 1 π bond. Each carbon retains one unhybridised 2p orbital, perpendicular to the plane; their sideways overlap, above and below the plane, is the π bond. C=C double bond, 1.34 Å. In the original model the angles were schematic; during conversion, the lobes and hydrogen atoms were placed at the hybridisation angle.

Parts of the model

  • C–C σ bond. The two sp² lobes pointing towards each other along the C–C axis. Their end-on (axial) overlap is the σ bond, common to all three molecules; together with the π bond, it forms the C=C double bond.
  • sp² hybrids towards H. The remaining sp² hybrid orbitals: two on each carbon, one for each C–H σ bond.
  • π bond (p orbitals). The two unhybridised 2p orbitals, one on each carbon, perpendicular to the molecular plane. The lines between them show the sideways overlap above and below the plane: this is the π bond.

Ethyne C₂H₂

sp hybridisation at each carbon: linear H–C≡C–H arrangement with an angle of 180°. 3 σ bonds (1 C–C and 2 C–H) and 2 π bonds. Each carbon retains two unhybridised 2p orbitals, perpendicular to each other; two π bonds form, with mutually perpendicular nodal planes. C≡C triple bond, 1.20 Å.

Parts of the model

  • C–C σ bond. The two sp lobes pointing towards each other along the C–C axis. Their end-on (axial) overlap is the σ bond, common to all three molecules; together with the two π bonds, it forms the C≡C triple bond.
  • sp hybrids towards H. The remaining sp hybrid orbitals: one on each carbon, one for each C–H σ bond.
