Linear ethyne molecule, H–C≡C–H, with a bond angle of 180° (sp hybridisation). In total, the molecule has 3 σ bonds (1 C–C and 2 C–H) and 2 π bonds. The C≡C triple bond consists of 1 σ + 2 π; the two pairs of p orbitals that form the π bonds have mutually perpendicular axes. In the model, the spheres represent atoms and the cylinders represent bonds; the triple bond is drawn as three cylinders. Bond lengths: C≡C 1.20 Å, C–H 1.06 Å.

Parts of the model

  • Carbon atoms. The two carbon atoms, which are sp-hybridised. The two sp hybrid orbitals of each carbon form the C–C and C–H σ bonds; the two unhybridised 2p orbitals form the two π bonds.
  • Hydrogen atoms. The two terminal hydrogen atoms. Each is bonded to one carbon by a σ bond (sp–1s overlap).
  • C≡C triple bond. Three cylinders between the carbons indicate a bond order of 3: one σ bond (sp–sp) and two π bonds (p–p). The cylinders do not correspond one-to-one to orbitals. Bond order 3, length 1.20 Å, shorter than C=C (1.34 Å) and C–C (1.54 Å).
  • C–H bonds. Two single σ bonds, one at each end of the molecule, each 1.06 Å long.
