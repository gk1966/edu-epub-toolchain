pako — high-speed zlib port to pure JavaScript

pako is bundled *inside* other minified vendor files rather than shipped as a
separate file. It is present inside `vendor/jszip.min.js` (JSZip uses pako for
DEFLATE/INFLATE) and, in the Creator, also inside `vendor/mammoth.browser.min.js`.

notice are reproduced here.
