sp hybridisation in ethyne (C2H2)

An interactive book to help you understand how the sp hybrid orbitals of carbon explain the linear structure and the triple bond of ethyne.

Author

Georgios Korakakis
Assistant Professor
Department of Graphic Design and Visual Communication
School of Applied Arts and Culture
University of West Attica

Who it is for

For students taking Chemistry in the final (third) year of the Greek Lyceum, upper secondary school (Science and Health Studies orientation group), and for teachers who want interactive material on hybridisation.

Your learning path

  1. Recall what σ and π bonds are.
  2. Understand why the concept of hybridisation is needed.
  3. Connect sp hybrid orbitals with the 180° angle and the linear structure.
  4. Examine σ and π overlap in ethyne using 3D models.
  5. Compare sp³, sp² and sp hybridisation.
  6. Check what you have learnt with questions that give immediate feedback.
