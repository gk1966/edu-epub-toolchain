Section 5

Energy diagrams for sp, sp² and sp³

In each type of hybridisation, carbon starts from the ground state, one 2s electron is promoted to the empty 2p orbital, and the orbitals are then combined. The more 2p orbitals take part, the closer the energy of the hybrids is to that of 2p, and the fewer unhybridised 2p orbitals remain for π bonds.

In the diagrams, the 2s, 2p and hybrid orbitals are distinguished in three ways: colour, fill pattern (none, dots, stripes) and label. The energies are qualitative, not to scale.
