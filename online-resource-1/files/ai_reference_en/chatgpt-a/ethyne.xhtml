What do we observe?

σ bond (sigma): formed by end-on overlap of orbitals along the axis joining the nuclei. In ethyne, each carbon is sp-hybridised. There are two C–H σ bonds and one C–C σ bond.

π bond (pi): formed by sideways overlap of parallel unhybridised p orbitals. In the C≡C triple bond there are two π bonds, formed by two pairs of p orbitals in mutually perpendicular directions.

Conclusion: C≡C = 1 σ bond + 2 π bonds. The H–C≡C–H molecule is linear, with bond angles of 180°.
