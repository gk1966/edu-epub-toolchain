Comparison: sp³, sp² and sp

Carbon can show three types of hybridisation. The number of p orbitals that are mixed determines how many hybrids form, the geometry and how many π bonds are possible.

The three types of carbon hybridisation
Propertysp³sp²sp
Orbitals mixedone 2s + three 2pone 2s + two 2pone 2s + one 2p
Hybrid orbitals432
Unhybridised p012
Arrangement / angletetrahedral, 109.5°trigonal planar, 120°linear, 180°
Bonds of C4 σ3 σ + 1 π2 σ + 2 π
ExampleCH4, CH3–CH3CH2=CH2HC≡CH

How to determine the hybridisation of a C atom

Count the σ bonds of the atom (each single, double or triple bond contains one σ bond). For the carbon atoms in the compounds studied here, the number of σ bonds = the number of hybrid orbitals: 4 σ → sp³, 3 σ → sp², 2 σ → sp.

Worked example: propene

C1H2=C2H–C3H3

C1one double + two single → 3 σ → sp²
C2one double + two single → 3 σ → sp²
C3four single → 4 σ → sp³
