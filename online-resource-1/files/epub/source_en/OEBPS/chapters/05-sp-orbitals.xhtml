From atomic orbitals to sp hybrids

On each carbon atom of ethyne, one 2s orbital and one 2p orbital combine mathematically to give two equivalent sp hybrid orbitals pointing in opposite directions.

The orbital “balance sheet” for one carbon atom

Mixedone 2s + one 2p
Producedtwo equivalent sp hybrid orbitals
Remainingtwo unhybridised 2p orbitals, perpendicular to each other and to the sp axis
Geometrylinear, with an angle of 180° between the two sp orbitals

A note on energy

An sp hybrid orbital has 50% s character and 50% p character. Its energy is higher than that of a pure s orbital and lower than that of a pure p orbital. This avoids the misconception that hybrid orbitals have the same energy as the original atomic orbitals.
