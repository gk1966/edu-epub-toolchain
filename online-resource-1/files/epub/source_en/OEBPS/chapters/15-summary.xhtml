Wrapping up

Summary and self-assessment

At a glance

  • σ bond: overlap along the axis. π bond: sideways overlap of parallel p orbitals.
  • sp hybridisation: one 2s + one 2p → two equivalent sp orbitals at 180°; two p orbitals remain.
  • Ethyne: linear, C≡C = 1 σ (sp–sp) + 2 π (p–p), plus two C–H σ bonds (sp–1s).
  • Rule: number of σ bonds of C = number of hybrid orbitals (4 → sp³, 3 → sp², 2 → sp).

For the teacher

  • Duration: about two teaching periods; the 3D chapters can also be set as homework.
  • Prerequisites: electron configuration of carbon; the covalent bond.
  • Suggestion: start with the chapter “σ and π bonds”, project the 3D comparison model in class and ask students to predict the geometry before they rotate the model.
  • Assessment: the questions give immediate feedback; the score is not saved.
